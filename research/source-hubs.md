# GentsAcademy learning-material source hubs

## Business

The University of Washington Libraries business OER guide identifies OpenStax Business, the Open Textbook Library business catalogue, OER Commons, MIT OpenCourseWare FinTech, MERLOT business case studies, and open textbooks in entrepreneurship, financial accounting, strategic management, and small-business financial management as suitable open or openly licensed sources. Source: https://guides.lib.uw.edu/oer/business

## Information technology

The California Community Colleges OERI IT/Information Systems collection is curated by a discipline lead and maps openly licensed materials to C-ID ITIS areas. It identifies OpenStax Foundations of Information Systems, OpenStax Workplace Software and Skills, MIT OpenCourseWare Introduction to Computer Science and Programming in Python, open networking texts, cybersecurity texts, cloud-computing open access books, and Cloud Security Alliance guidance. Source: https://asccc-oeri.org/open-educational-resources-and-information-technology-and-information-systems-itis/

## Hospitality and tourism

The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley hospitality and tourism guide identifies open textbooks on cultural heritage tourism and event planning, food production and service, introductory tourism and hospitality, workplace safety in foodservice, MERLOT hospitality and tourism resources, and open-access journals. Source: https://utrgv.libguides.com/hospitality-and-tourism-management/OER

The University of Central Florida hospitality OER guide confirms that OER can be public-domain or openly licensed, and links to OER Commons, Open Textbook Library, MERLOT, OpenStax, Pressbooks, and open business cases. Source: https://guides.ucf.edu/OERforHospitality

## University and professor video sources

Initial official sources include Oxford Saïd Business School’s Building a Business lecture series, Stanford GSB Business Videos, Stanford Online, MIT OpenCourseWare video lectures, Professor Dimitrios Buhalis’s tourism and smart-tourism channel, and university tourism/hospitality lecture material. Video links will be selected per course and week, retaining the originating university, professor, title, URL, and access date in the material manifest.

Research rule: do not copy proprietary textbooks or download copyrighted lecture files. Create original GentsAcademy study guides with attributed references and link or embed official public videos. PDFs will contain original explanations, diagrams, learning objectives, activities, references, and source licenses or access notes.
